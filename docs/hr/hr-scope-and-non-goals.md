\# BMS Enterprise Suite — HR Scope \& Non-Goals (Authoritative)



\## 1. Purpose



This document defines the scope and explicit non-goals

of the HR module in BMS Enterprise Suite.



It prevents HR from becoming an uncontrolled data silo.



This document is normative.



---



\## 2. HR Module Scope (What HR IS)



The HR module is responsible for:



\- Employee master records

\- Employment status (active, suspended, terminated)

\- Contract metadata (type, start, end)

\- Role assignments (business roles, not system roles)

\- Organizational structure (departments, reporting lines)



HR data is:

\- Sensitive

\- Auditable

\- Subject to retention rules



---



\## 3. Explicit Non-Goals (What HR IS NOT)



The HR module MUST NOT:



\- Handle payroll calculations

\- Store salary amounts in Phase 0

\- Execute payments

\- Replace accounting or finance

\- Manage system authentication directly

\- Override governance approvals



Payroll may be integrated later as a separate program.



---



\## 4. Authority Boundaries



\- HR owns employee truth

\- Finance owns compensation truth

\- IT owns system access

\- Governance owns approvals



No module may overstep its boundary.



---



\## 5. Compliance Expectations



HR data must support:

\- Access logging

\- Role-based visibility

\- Historical record retention

\- Right-to-know enforcement



---



\## 6. Final Statement



HR exists to manage people,

not to bypass controls.



A controlled HR module enables growth.

An uncontrolled one creates risk.



End of document.
