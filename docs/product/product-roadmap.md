\# BMS Enterprise Suite — Product Roadmap (Authoritative)



\## 1. Purpose



This document defines the authoritative product roadmap for

BMS Enterprise Suite.



It establishes:

\- What is already complete

\- What is planned next

\- The correct sequencing of future modules



This document is normative.



---



\## 2. Completed Scope (Frozen)



The following scope is complete and frozen:



\- Core financial modules (Customers, Sales, Invoices, Payments, AR, Ledger)

\- Financial periods and close discipline

\- Reporting and KPIs

\- Snapshot generation and governance

\- Audit vault and immutability

\- Identity, roles, and authority enforcement

\- Operational runbooks and compliance artifacts



No functional changes are permitted without governance review.



---



\## 3. Near-Term Enhancements (Same Domain)



These enhancements improve usability and clarity without adding new domains:



\- UI clarity between live data and snapshot data

\- Explicit period labels across all reports

\- Snapshot awareness indicators in reports

\- Performance hardening and stability

\- Operational tooling improvements (read-only)



These do not change core behavior.



---



\## 4. Next Major Program (Planned, Not Started)



\### Program: HR Management \& Payroll



Planned scope:

\- Employees

\- Contracts

\- Attendance

\- Leave management

\- Payroll preparation

\- Payroll snapshots and approvals

\- HR audit trail

\- Finance integration (ledger posting)



Status:

\- NOT STARTED

\- Requires separate program declaration



---



\## 5. Subsequent Programs (Deferred)



After HR, the following programs are planned:



\### Inventory \& Operations

\- Products and services

\- Stock management

\- Purchasing

\- Supplier management

\- Cost tracking



\### Tax \& Compliance

\- VAT rules

\- Tax reporting

\- Regulatory exports



\### Platform Expansion

\- Multi-company

\- Multi-currency

\- Data isolation



---



\## 6. Sequencing Rules



\- Only one major program is active at a time

\- Core finance remains authoritative

\- New domains integrate via ledger and periods

\- Snapshots and governance apply uniformly



---



\## 7. Final Statement



This roadmap ensures that BMS Enterprise Suite evolves

without sacrificing correctness, auditability, or trust.



Nothing is rushed.

Nothing is forgotten.

Everything is sequenced.



End of document.
